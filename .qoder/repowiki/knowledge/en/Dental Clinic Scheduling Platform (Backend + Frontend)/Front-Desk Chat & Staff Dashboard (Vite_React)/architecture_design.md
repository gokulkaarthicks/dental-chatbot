Two top-level pages coexist under `src/pages/` -- `OverviewPage.tsx` (patient) and `pages/staff/StaffApp.tsx` (receptionist) -- both bootstrapped from `src/main.tsx`. The two pages do not import each other; cross-child wiring is enforced through the shared layer: `api/client.ts` and `api/staff.ts` for HTTP, `hooks/` for chat lifecycle/card/session state, and `lib/sessionStore.ts` for tab-scoped persistence. Reusable UI primitives live under `components/` and are consumed by both patient and staff pages, while styles are centralized in `styles/global.css` plus component-scoped CSS files. The build output under `dist/` is served by nginx via the bundled `nginx.conf`.
